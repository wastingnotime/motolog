of 2026-11-12. The original handoff and its private location are not published.
The [Fazer YS250 BlueFlex review](fazer-ys250-blueflex-maintenance.md) records

to the MotoLog bike remains pending; no maintenance baseline is seeded.

## Maintenance source admission

Before adding a baseline, verify the manual's exact model, year range, market,
identity and edition. Do not infer applicability from the Fazer name alone.
Publish only an original factual maintenance summary, with nonpersonal metadata:

- Manufacturer, document title/identifier, edition and supported model/year/market.
- Relevant printed page and table identifier; distinguish PDF page index if needed.
- SHA-256 of the reviewed source bytes, plus verification status and limitations.

Do not invent missing metadata, intervals or applicability. If evidence is
insufficient, leave the value unseeded and document the gap. Preserve original
baselines separately from owner overrides and identify owner changes as **Custom**.
The linked review records its source hash, verification limits and unseeded facts;
it does not establish a completed-service baseline.

## Publication boundary

Keep source PDFs, screenshots, rendered pages, extracted text, manual quotations,
owner vehicle documents, identifiers, actual service/fuel/ride records and local
absolute evidence paths outside public commits. Public summaries must be original
factual writing, not copied tables or a reconstructed manual. Private evidence
locators and review evidence belong in local task records.

Do not import private sibling product artifacts or proprietary policies. Repository
licensing does not grant redistribution rights to third-party source materials.
Review staged files and their content before publication.
