## Reading and schedule semantics

The edition page and printed 6-3 through 6-10 (PDF 52-59) were rendered privately
and visually read, including row boundaries, separate action rows, blank cells,
the 1,000/5,000/10,000-km columns, the recurring column and both pages of notes.
This review is independent of the earlier four replacement candidates.

Printed 6-3 / PDF 52, introductory notes, distinguishes the periodic inspection
cadence (5,000 km or six months, subject to the mileage-service qualification)
from item-specific replacements. It directs replay of the schedule from its
5,000-km column starting at 15,000 km. These are odometer milestones, not a
universal 5,000-km replacement recurrence. A blank cell supplies no service.
For dual thresholds, MotoLog's first-threshold rule applies; a completed mileage
service must be reconciled with the note rather than creating duplicate work.

The following anchor categories describe evidence, **not an implemented engine**:

- **Milestone:** retain the original odometer columns and replay instruction.
  Late completion must not silently move later manual milestones. The manual
  does not settle MotoLog's postponement/reconciliation behavior or the initial
  calendar anchor; both remain planning decisions.
- **Interval:** the source gives a distance or elapsed-time frequency, but does
  not define a retrospective anchor for a used bike. Keep the last qualifying
  replacement's date/odometer unknown until evidenced. Whether completion resets
  the target or a fixed original schedule is retained requires an explicit
  product decision; no synthetic zero, acquisition date or manufacture date is
  substituted. Initial calendar origin is unverified.
- **Event/condition:** keep the event or condition independently actionable;
  do not translate it into an invented distance/time interval. Missing condition
  observations mean unknown status.

Coverage is **partial**. The selected facts below are an original, limited
summary, not the manual's checklist, repair procedures or table arrangement.
Every entry is unseeded pending applicability and scheduling decisions. Source
verification does not mean automatic evaluation is supported.

## Oil and oil-filter milestones

These actions use **milestone** anchors and stay separate. No additional
event/condition trigger is quantified for them in the reviewed table/note:

- **Engine-oil replacement:** first marked at 1,000 km, then at 5,000 and
  10,000 km, with the 5,000-km/six-month recurring column also marked. The replay
  note gives later mileage visits at 15,000, 20,000 km and onward. There is no
  distinct first-service date in these pages. Source verified: general table,
  item 20, printed 6-8 / PDF 57; introductory note, printed 6-3 / PDF 52.
- **Oil-level/leak inspection:** item 20 includes this action under the same
  marked columns. Retain a separate inspection outcome; an inspection cannot
  complete an oil replacement. This is the periodic-table coverage only, not
  exhaustive daily/pre-ride guidance. Same locators and anchor category.
- **Oil-filter-element replacement:** marks are at 1,000 and 10,000 km;
  the 5,000-km and recurring-column cells are blank. Replaying the 5,000/10,000
  columns from 15,000 km yields later replacements at 20,000, 30,000 km, etc.
  This later sequence is derived from the note, not a mark in the recurring
  column. Do not invent a six-month filter replacement or change the first
  1,000-km milestone into a repeating interval. Source verified: general table,
  item 21, printed 6-8 / PDF 57; replay note, printed 6-3 / PDF 52.

Automatic evaluation is deferred for all three until first-service handling,
calendar origins, replay and late/partial completion are designed. An oil change
does not establish a filter replacement, and neither supplies missing prior work.

## Conditional care and separate inspections

- **Air-filter-element replacement:** general table item 1, printed 6-5 /
  PDF 54, gives 40,000 km with no separate first-service exception or time
  threshold. The air-filter note, printed 6-9 / PDF 58, requires more frequent
  replacement in humid or dusty use without quantifying the shorter interval.
  Source verified; **interval plus condition** anchors. Condition-aware
  evaluation is deferred; 40,000 km alone must not imply all use is covered.
  Do not confuse element replacement with cleaning its separate check hose.
- **Drive-chain inspection, adjustment and lubrication:** general table item
  12, printed 6-7 / PDF 56, specifies 500 km and after washing or riding in rain.
  No separate first-service exception or calendar threshold is supplied.
  Source verified; **interval plus event** anchors, with slack, alignment and
  condition inspection retained alongside care. Event handling and qualifying
  care anchors are unresolved. A wash/rain event must not disappear because
  500 km has not elapsed; no chain-replacement interval is inferred.
- **Spark-plug inspection/cleaning/gap correction:** emissions table item 3,
  inspection subrow, printed 6-4 / PDF 53, marks 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000 km and
  the 5,000-km/six-month recurring column. Source verified; **milestone** anchors
  and replay note apply (printed 6-3 / PDF 52). No additional event trigger or
  distinct first-service date is specified there. This never completes plug
  replacement; automatic evaluation is deferred with other table inspections.
- **Brake-hose inspection:** general table item 6, inspection subrow, printed
  6-6 / PDF 55, marks 5,000 and 10,000 km and the recurring column, leaving
  1,000 km blank. Source verified; **milestone** anchors and replay note apply
  (printed 6-3 / PDF 52). Inspect damage and routing/attachment separately from
  four-year replacement; damage also triggers replacement as noted above.
  No separate first-service date is supplied; automatic inspection evaluation
  is deferred.
- **Hydraulic-brake checks:** general table items 4 and 5, printed 6-5 / PDF 54,
  mark front/rear operation, fluid-level and leak checks at 1,000, 5,000 and
  10,000 km and the recurring column. Source verified; **milestone** anchors
  and replay note apply (printed 6-3 / PDF 52). The hydraulic note at printed
  6-9 / PDF 58 also calls for regular level checks/correction, without a numeric
  extra interval. No distinct first-service date is supplied. Regular-check
  timing remains unquantified; checks or topping up do not complete fluid or
  internal-parts replacement. Automatic evaluation is deferred.
