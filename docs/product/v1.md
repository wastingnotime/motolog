# MotoLog V1 product brief

## Confirmed intent

MotoLog is an Android-only, local-first companion for one owner and one Yamaha
Fazer 250 in the 2016/2017 target range. Real riding experience should inform
refinement without publishing the owner's records. It is independent of Rastro.
Registration, recording, tracking, histories and core dashboard calculations
must work offline.

V1 excludes multiple motorcycles, rider profiles, accounts, workshops, mechanic
workflows, marketplaces, a general motorcycle catalog, cloud sync, backend
dependencies, iOS and web. This is product intent, not implementation approval.

## Registration and maintenance sources

Register the supported motorcycle through a preset and associate every fuel,
maintenance and ride record with its stable identity. The single-bike limit is
a V1 constraint.

Preventive templates must come from an applicable owner's manual. Verify exact
model, market and year before seeding; record source identity, edition and the
relevant page or table for each baseline rule. Preserve the manual baseline and
owner overrides separately. Unverified values stay unseeded and missing data
must be visible. Never infer intervals from a different Fazer variant.

Checklist items and schedules are editable. Owner-added or modified items,
including interval overrides, display **Custom** and retain a visible source.
Keep a historical snapshot of completed work and its provenance as a planning
proposal until its representation is agreed.

## Maintenance behavior

- Completed work has a read-only chronological history. Template edits,
  postponements and subsequent revisions cannot rewrite it.
- A preventive revision is due at either its mileage or elapsed-time threshold,
  whichever occurs first. Use the sole threshold when only one is defined.
  Preserve original targets after postponement.
- Full performance of a preventive revision or programmed job records completed
  work in history and clears that pending occurrence.
- Full postponement reschedules the entire checklist and records no completed work.
- Partial postponement records only performed items and creates linked outstanding
  work for the remainder with a new due target. Save both outcomes together;
  repeated actions must not duplicate work or corrupt completion state.
- Outstanding work stays visible until performed. Programmed maintenance can be
  scheduled and postponed without disabling ride tracking.
- Corrective records include service date, location, issue/description, cost,
  motorcycle odometer, performed work and replacements, and appear in history.

## Fuel and measurement authority

Both refuel entry modes create the same event and provide history showing the
recorded odometer:

| Entered values | Derived value |
| --- | --- |
| Liters, total paid, odometer | Unit price = total paid / liters |
| Total paid, price per liter, odometer | Liters = total paid / unit price |

Label total paid and price per liter distinctly. Retaining entered values,
rounding, validation and backdated-entry behavior need planning decisions.

The owner-entered motorcycle odometer is authoritative for maintenance mileage
and refueling. GPS supplies tracked ride distance and speed. GPS must never
silently replace or overwrite an odometer reading.

Kilometers since refuel requires a suitable current odometer reading and the
latest refuel's recorded odometer. Show missing current readings explicitly.
Any future GPS estimate must be separately labeled and preserve its source.

## Rides and dashboard

Provide manual start/stop and optional automatic start/stop, with explicit day
boundary behavior. Record ride distance and speed variation using an agreed
speed-change threshold. Threshold semantics/configurability and automatic
start/stop criteria require decisions before implementation.

Use large manual start/stop and refuel controls. Show recording state and
automatic mode. Display today's average speed, today's distance, kilometers
since refuel and the last refuel odometer; explicitly represent unavailable
statistics.

Design dashboard and quick refuel actions for use while safely stopped during a
ride. Registration, settings, maintenance and detailed histories are parked
management flows. Motion-based interaction lockout remains a design decision.

Core records live on the device. Permission denial, missing GPS fixes and
tracking interruptions must preserve saved records and expose gaps. Never
fabricate speed or distance.
